 BEFORE YOU ENTER THE LAB

How to play The Petsperiment.

Get ready

Each player gets a board, chooses a target Creature and starts with 4 Action cards. Everyone can see the Experiment sequence on each board.

Your turn

  1. Draw an Experiment: if it matches the next Experiment required by your sequence, place it; otherwise discard it. Events trigger immediately.
  2. Draw an Action card.
  3. Play one Action or pass. You can have at most 7 cards in your hand at the end of your turn.

How to win

Complete the three Experiments in the order shown on your board and place a Creature. Your target Creature wins immediately. A different Creature opens a reaction window: the others can still intervene, including in a chain of reactions.

The exceptions that cause chaos

Steal Creature and Discard Creature are played out of turn when an opponent is about to win. Reverse is played at the start of your own turn and reverses its phases. There is only one copy of each of the three purple Actions, and they leave the game after use.

The Microscope has four different uses plus a defence. The Scientists and Archive modules are optional and can be added separately or together.
