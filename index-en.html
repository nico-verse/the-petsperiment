Game Identity

Easy to learn. Hard to put down.

You're a scientist: complete your sequence and summon your creature before everyone else. But your board is out in the open for everyone to see — and they'll all do everything they can to stop you. Learn it in a minute, play a round in twenty.

For genre fans: a high-interaction take-that card filler, with optional advanced modules.

Every player is a scientist who must complete their sequence and summon their creature. The board is always visible to everyone: everyone knows when you're about to win — and when to strike.
Objective

3 colors in the exact order + 1 Creature.

The first player to fill all 4 slots on their board wins. The creature chosen at the start of the game can make the win automatic.

The board

Setup and Turn

How a turn works

Each player takes a board, chooses a target Creature, the two decks are shuffled separately and everyone draws 4 Action cards. The player who looks most like a scientist goes first; ties go to the youngest.

Setup

Board + Creature

The color sequence is visible to everyone. The target Creature is your personal starting choice.

Phase 1

Draw Experiment

If it's the color of your next slot, you place it; otherwise it goes to the discard pile.

Phase 2

Draw Action

Add a card to your hand. Maximum 7 cards at the end of the turn.

Phase 3

Play / Pass

Play a single Action, or pass.

The Cards

The game's cards

Every card is illustrated and shows its own effect. You'll find a selection of them in the gallery below. Only one Action card per turn, except for the exceptions the rules allow.

Actions

Microscope, Discard/Steal Experiment, Skip Turn, Skip Draw, Swap, Draw Experiment. The Microscope is the most versatile: 4 uses plus a defense.

Out-of-turn Actions

Steal Creature and Discard Creature are played out of turn when an opponent is about to win. Reverse flips the phases of your turn. Extremely rare: they leave the game for good after use.

Event Cards

KAOS, BOOM, Vertigo

They stay in the Experiment deck but aren't placed: they trigger the moment they're drawn and can flip the game around.

Reaction Window

When someone is about to win, the game can still change.

If the drawn Creature isn't the target one, the following players in turn order can use Steal or Discard Creature. A chain of reactions is possible. Whoever is the target of Skip Turn can't react.

Advanced Modules

Play with Scientists and Archive mode.

Archive and Scientists can be added separately or together to the base game, for a deeper, more strategic match.

Archive Mode

Unhelpful color cards can be kept in 1 personal slot, visible to everyone, to trigger its effect at the right moment.

27 cards, 8 different effects to discover by playing.

Scientists Mode

Each player gets 1 random Scientist, with an ability that triggers when you have the right colors on your board.

12 Scientists, 12 secret abilities.

Archive — Effects

27 color cards, 8 secret effect types.

Every Archive color card hides one of 8 possible different effects, spread across the 9 red, 9 yellow and 9 blue cards. You'll discover which by playing — or by following us on social media for a few hints.

Scientists

12 Scientists, 12 secret abilities.

Advanced module. Each player gets 1 random Scientist. The ability triggers when you have the right colors on your board — which ones exactly, and what they do, you'll discover by playing.

A taste: you'll find 2 of them in the gallery above. The other 10 stay a secret — follow us on social media for previews.
